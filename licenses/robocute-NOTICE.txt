The shared BSDF foundations in
`crates/prime-vulkan/shaders/bsdf/common/common.slang` and
`crates/prime-vulkan/shaders/bsdf/common/material.slang` are adapted from legacy
Prime's compact boundary utilities. Legacy Prime identifies the mathematical
basis of those utilities with the BSDF implementation distributed by RoboCute:

https://github.com/RoboCute/RoboCute

BSDF base source revision recorded by legacy Prime:
5985e989254b4685e3885d876b33f4874d233dcd

Copyright RoboCute contributors. Licensed under the Apache License, Version
2.0. The unmodified license is included in robocute-Apache-2.0.txt.

This port retains the common value types, event flags, scalar/vector helpers,
frame construction, Fresnel/IOR and roughness relations, volume derivation,
bounded stack operations and default material initialization. Module names,
visibility and imports are adapted to Prime PT's explicit LitePBR interface.

The incorporated implementation is Prime's low-order LitePBR, carried by
legacy Prime revision ca364b25c4b4c7de5c8eae82115b401bf1932b3e. This notice does
not describe a complete compact OpenPBR closure library, an author hotfix
source overlay, or transmission-GGX energy data. The LitePBR shader and Prime
integration retain their separate project copyright and license.

The LitePBR adaptation explicitly returns R=1 and T=0 at the thin-wall Snell TIR
endpoint and removes a redundant incident-side sign factor from the initial
refractive reflection-candidate guard, retaining exit reflection and TIR.
Half-vector orientation, subsequent support checks, relative eta and medium
state are preserved; the other source math is retained.
