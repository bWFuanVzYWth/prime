Prime's epipolar sun-shadow profile is adapted from the reference's
per-epipolar-slice shadow-map line and one-dimensional min/max hierarchy.
Prime replaces the raster cascaded shadow maps, screen-space interpolation,
and one-dimensional texture hierarchy with a conservative profile over
Prime's world-space ray-traced directional-shadow clipmaps. Uncovered,
overflowed, or otherwise unknown profile intervals remain shadowed.

Prime PT uses demand-filled leaf columns with the same conservative profile.
